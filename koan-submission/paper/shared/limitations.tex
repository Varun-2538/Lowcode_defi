\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Local EVM, not a mainnet fork.} The execution layer deploys a
constant-product AMM and mock tokens on an in-process EVM with
deterministically seeded reserves. This yields real EVM execution
semantics (real transfers, reverts, and gas) and byte-for-byte
reproducibility, but the prices and liquidity are synthetic. It is a
pinned local snapshot rather than a live mainnet fork; absolute
price-impact magnitudes depend on the seeded reserves, though the
qualitative unsafe-execution finding does not. Replacing the synthetic AMM
with a mainnet-fork RPC is a natural extension and the harness interface
is designed for that substitution.

\paragraph{Scale, single annotator, and scoring strictness.} The development
split uses 120 expert-authored prompts and the held-out split $87$, with
temperature-$0$ decoding and a single run per model on two model families.
Temperature-$0$ API calls still vary slightly across runs, so individual
decimals are approximate; we report every rate with a confidence interval and
generate all tables from saved outputs. The gold annotations were authored
primarily by one team; we provide a second-annotator protocol, a reproducible
stratified subset, and tooling to compute inter-annotator agreement, but the
agreement number awaits an independent pass and is therefore not yet reported.
Edge matching is strict and type-level, which penalizes reasonable-but-reordered
graphs and treats structural rates as a lower bound; this does not affect the
safe-rate or the on-chain unsafe-execution findings, which are driven by
missing safety parameterization rather than edge ordering.

\paragraph{Scope of the Koan-Safe method.} Koan-Safe encodes engineered domain
knowledge---a token dictionary, per-category node presets, and conservative
safety defaults---and reads only the raw prompt, but it has no access to gold
annotations. Its enforcement layer improves safety, not intent extraction: it
never fabricates a missing amount, price, or chain, so its achievable
executable rate is bounded by what the parser and generator can read from the
prompt. Its rule-based generator's fixed presets do not cover workflow
structures outside the four categories, which is visible as reduced structural
validity on the held-out variants; the language-model and hybrid generators
mitigate but do not fully remove this. The held-out split is authored by the
same team as the method, so it tests structural and surface generalization but
not adversarial distribution shift from an independent party.

\paragraph{Category scope of the execution layer.} On-chain execution
currently covers swaps and the swap leg of compositional workflows; limit
orders are checked for immediate fillability against the pool mid-price,
and cross-chain workflows are not executed because a single local chain
cannot bridge. Every unexecuted case is recorded explicitly rather than
silently passed.
